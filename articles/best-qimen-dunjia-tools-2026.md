# Best Qi Men Dunjia Tools in 2026: Five Dimensions, No Unverifiable Rankings

Most "best Qimen Dunjia tools" lists are affiliate pages with a star rating and no method. A rating
you cannot audit is marketing, not a recommendation. So instead of a ranking, here are the five
dimensions that actually separate a trustworthy tool from a pretty one — each with a self-test you
can run in ten minutes.

## 1. Chart correctness — the calendar layer

The chart is only as good as the calendar under it. Three things must be right before any
interpretation matters:

- **True solar time** conversion (longitude + equation of time), not just clock time.
- **Dingju method** stated explicitly — 拆补 (split-patch) and 置闰 (intercalary) can produce
  *different* charts for the same minute.
- **Yin/Yang Dun** switched by solar term, not by a fixed month table.

**Self-test:** cast the same date/time on three tools. If the 局数, 九星, or 八门 differ and the tool
cannot explain why, you have found your answer.

## 2. Ruleset visibility

A tool that hides its method cannot be audited, and a reading you cannot audit is a horoscope. Good
tools print the method on the chart: which 定局 school, which 寄宫 convention (天禽 to 天芮 or 天柱),
whether 转盘 only, and how 空亡 is calculated.

## 3. Interpretation auditability

Every sentence in a reading should point back to a symbol in *your* chart — a 宫, a 星, a 门, a 神,
a 干. "You are feeling blocked" is not a reading. "The Life Door sits in the Kun palace with the
Snake, void by 旬空" is. Ask any AI tool to show its work; the ones that can are the ones worth
keeping.

## 4. Validation against classical cases

Ask whether the engine has been tested against documented cases from the classics and published
regression results. Ours: 61 cases drawn from classical texts, all matching; a judgement baseline of
11/12. If a vendor will not state a number, assume there is no test suite.

## 5. The free tier, honestly

Free is a business model, not a moral position. The honest question is *what* is free: chart casting
and the raw chart should be free forever (it is deterministic); AI interpretation and follow-up
questions are what cost money. A tool that hides the chart behind a login is monetising the part
that should never be gated.

## The five-question checklist

1. Does it show true solar time and the 定局 method?
2. Can I see every symbol the reading references?
3. Does the AI explanation cite the palace it read from?
4. Is there any published accuracy or regression result?
5. What exactly is free, and what is paid?
