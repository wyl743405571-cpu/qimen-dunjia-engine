# Free Divination Without Signup: What You Actually Get (and Don't)

Almost every "free reading" site asks for an email before it shows you anything. That is not
generosity — it is lead generation. So what can a genuinely no-signup tool give you, and where does
the honest limit sit?

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/free-divination-no-signup.html). This is a condensed version.*

## Why nearly every site wants your email

A free chart costs the vendor almost nothing to compute. The email is the product: it feeds
retargeting, newsletters, and "your reading is ready, subscribe to unlock the full version" funnels.
The gate is not technical — it is commercial.

## What a real no-signup tool gives you

- **The chart itself, fully.** Year, month, day, hour pillars; the nine palaces; 九星, 八门, 八神,
  天干; 空亡; the 定局 method stated.
- **A basic interpretation** of the structure, palace by palace.
- **No account, no cookie wall, no "verify your email to continue".**

## What free and anonymous cannot give you

This is the part the marketing omits:

- **Continuity.** No saved chart history — keep your own screenshots.
- **Unlimited volume.** Even honest tools rate-limit (ours: 3 readings/month anonymous, 5 registered,
  50 paid). Computing is cheap; interpretation is not.
- **Deep follow-up.** The value of a good reading is the back-and-forth ("what if I wait three
  weeks?"). Follow-up is where the real cost sits and where a paid tier is legitimate.

## Is no-signup actually viable at scale?

Yes — if the business model is *paid deep readings*, not *selling user data*. The test is simple:
does the site gate the deterministic part (the chart), or the expensive part (the AI interpretation
and follow-up)? Gating the chart is a warning sign. Gating the interpretation is normal.

## Try before you register

The sane sequence for any tool:

1. Cast one chart anonymously. Check whether true solar time and the 定局 method are shown.
2. Ask one interpretation question. See whether the answer cites the palace it read from.
3. Only then decide whether the paid depth is worth it.

If a tool will not let you do step 1 without an email, it has already told you what it values.
