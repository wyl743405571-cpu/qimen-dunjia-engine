# The Eight Gods of Qimen Dunjia: Invisible Forces and When to Heed Them

Every Qimen chart carries eight 神 (gods / deities) — not beings to worship, but **eight recurring
patterns of force** that colour the palace they sit in. They are the "weather" of a situation: the
same palace reads differently under the Chief than under the Snake.

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/eight-gods-invisible-forces.html). This is a condensed version.*

## Where the gods sit

One god is attached to each of the eight outer palaces, moving with the 值符 (Chief) each 时辰. The
god does not act alone — it modifies the star, gate, and stems in the same palace. Read it as an
adjective, not a verdict.

## The eight, one by one

| God | Chinese | Force | Heed it when | Treat as noise when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chief | 值符 | Authority, legitimacy, the "official" line | You need backing, a formal channel, an authority's approval | You are acting privately and informally |
| Snake | 腾蛇 | Entanglement, illusion, anxiety loops | Something feels sticky, unclear, or over-thought | You are already decisive and the facts are plain |
| Moon | 太阴 | Concealment, planning, the hidden hand | Quiet preparation, research, backstage work | The matter needs open, public action |
| Harmony | 六合 | Connection, negotiation, matchmaking | Partnerships, contracts, introductions, mediation | You need to act alone and fast |
| Tiger | 白虎 | Force, injury, urgency, confrontation | Conflict, surgery, legal disputes, hard deadlines | You need softness and patience |
| Void | 玄武 | Deception, theft, ambiguity, missing information | Documents, money, trust, anything with a hidden party | Everything is transparent and verified |
| Earth | 九地 | Stability, storage, defence, inertia | Holding position, defending, waiting out a cycle | You need movement and speed |
| Sky | 九天 | Expansion, ambition, distance, visibility | Launches, travel, going public, scaling | You need caution and consolidation |

## The practical rule

A god is a **modifier**, never a command. A chart with the Chief in your palace is not "you will
win"; it means the structure favours legitimate, above-board action. A Snake in the palace is not
"disaster"; it means the situation will reward patience and distrust of surface clarity.

Read the god *together with* the gate (开门 speaks of openings, 死门 of closure) and the star (天芮
of trouble, 天辅 of support). One symbol alone is never the answer.

## How to use this in a reading

1. Find the palace your question sits in (usually the Day Stem's palace, or the palace of the
   relevant 用神).
2. Note the god in that palace.
3. Ask: does this god *amplify* or *complicate* the gate and star already there?
4. Translate it into a concrete posture — wait, verify, negotiate, defend, or move.

The gods are a vocabulary for the invisible. Used this way they sharpen a decision; used as
fortune-telling they just add noise.
