# The Four Components of a Qimen Chart: A Beginner's Map

A Qimen Dunjia chart looks like a wall of characters the first time you see it. It is not
unstructured — it has exactly **four layers**, and they are read in a fixed order. Learn the four and
the wall becomes a sentence.

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/four-components-qimen-chart.html). This is a condensed version.*

## Layer 1 — The Ju number (局数): which "game script" is running

The chart is one of 1,080 possible configurations (18 局 × 10 stems × 12 hours in the classical
count, compressed by the 定局 method). The 局数 tells you the *overall posture*: yang or yin dun,
ascending or descending, which palace the Chief starts in. It is the script, before any actor speaks.

**Read it as:** is this a period of expansion or consolidation? Which direction is the momentum
running?

## Layer 2 — The nine palaces (九宫): the board itself

The 3×3 Luoshu grid. Each palace is a territory with an element, a direction, and a life domain
(career, wealth, authority, speech, etc.). This is the board the actors stand on — and it is why
Qimen can speak about *where*, not just *when*.

**Read it as:** which territory does my question live in, and what is the palace opposite it (the
tension point)?

## Layer 3 — Stars, gates, and deities: the actors on the board

Three symbol families sit in each palace:

- **九星 (nine stars)** — the *quality* of the influence: 天辅 support, 天芮 trouble, 天冲 momentum,
  and so on.
- **八门 (eight gates)** — the *access*: 开门 an opening, 休门 rest, 生门 livelihood, 死门 closure,
  杜门 blockage, 景门 appearance, 惊门 alarm, 伤门 harm.
- **八神 (eight deities)** — the *flavour*: Chief (authority), Snake (entanglement), Moon
  (concealment), Harmony (connection), Tiger (force), Void (deception), Earth (stability), Sky
  (expansion).

**Read them as:** who is present, what door they came through, and in what mood. One symbol alone is
never the answer; the reading is the *combination*.

## Layer 4 — Heaven stems and earth stems (天盘/地盘): the relationship wiring

Each palace carries a 天盘 stem (the active, moving influence) above a 地盘 stem (the fixed base).
Their relationship — generating, controlling, combining, clashing — is the **wiring** of the chart.
This layer is where most of the actual judgement happens: the same gate reads differently depending
on which stem it is carrying.

**Read it as:** what is acting on what, and is the relationship supportive or abrasive?

## The order to read them in

1. **Board** — locate the palace of your question.
2. **Bureau** — what is the overall posture of this period?
3. **Pattern** — stars, gates, deities in that palace (and its opposite).
4. **Response** — the stems' relationship, plus timing and direction.

Read out of order and you will drown in symbols. Read in order and each layer narrows the question.

## What this map does not claim

It does not tell you what to choose. It gives you a structure — where the opening is, what is
blocking it, and when the window moves. The decision still belongs to you.
