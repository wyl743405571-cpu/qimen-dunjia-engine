# How to Read a Qimen Chart in Four Layers: A Worked Walkthrough

Most beginners stall at the same place: they can cast a chart, but they cannot *read* one. The fix is
a fixed order. Read out of order and you drown; read in order and every step narrows the question.

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/how-to-read-a-qimen-chart.html). This is a condensed version with a worked example.*

## Step 0 — Ask the right question (before any symbol)

A chart cannot answer a vague question. "What should I do with my life?" returns noise. "Should I
accept this offer this month, given that it means relocating?" returns a structure.

Name three things before you cast: **the object** (what decision), **the time window** (this week /
this month), and **what you are actually choosing between**.

## Layer 1 — The board (盘): which palace is the matter in?

Locate the palace your question sits in. Usually that is the palace of the Day Stem (日干, "you"), or
the palace of the relevant 用神 (the symbol for the thing asked about — 开门 for career, 生门 for
livelihood, 六合 for a relationship).

This step tells you **which territory of life** the question actually lives in — which is often not
the one you assumed.

## Layer 2 — The bureau (局): what is the overall posture?

Before reading any single symbol, read the 局: yin or yang dun, which palace the Chief starts in,
whether the chart is expanding or consolidating. This is the "weather" of the period. A good gate in
a consolidating chart means "move carefully"; the same gate in an expanding chart means "go now".

## Layer 3 — The pattern (格): stems' relationship

Now the symbols inside the palace: the star (quality), the gate (access), the deity (flavour), and —
most importantly — the **heaven stem over the earth stem** (天盘/地盘). Their relationship (生成,
克制, 合, 冲) is where the actual judgement lives.

A gate is not good or bad by itself. 开门 in a palace whose stems are in conflict is an opening with
friction; 死门 with generating stems can mean a "closed" door that is actually protecting you.

## Layer 4 — The response (应): timing and direction

Finally, the 应: which time windows the structure activates, and which direction it favours. This is
what turns a reading into something actionable — *not* "it will work out", but "the window opens in
the next 旬, approach from the north".

## Worked example: "should I accept this offer?"

1. **Question:** accept a job offer that requires relocating, decide within two weeks.
2. **Board:** Day Stem sits in 乾 — authority and structure; the offer is real and formal, not vague.
3. **Bureau:** consolidating — the period favours securing position, not gambling.
4. **Pattern:** 开门 (open door) in the same palace, but the heaven stem clashes with the earth stem —
   the opening exists *with friction* (likely the relocation or the terms).
5. **Response:** the window tightens in the next 旬; the favourable direction is northwest — which
   happens to be where the new office sits.

**Reading:** the door is open but abrasive. The honest move is not "yes" or "no" — it is "negotiate
the friction before the window closes". That is what a structured chart gives you: a specific
negotiation, not a verdict.

## What a chart cannot tell you

- Whether you *want* it (that is yours).
- The other party's private intentions.
- Anything about health, law, or money that requires a professional.
- A guarantee. A chart maps structure; it does not promise outcomes.
