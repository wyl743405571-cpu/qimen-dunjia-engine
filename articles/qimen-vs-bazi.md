# Qi Men Dunjia vs Bazi: Two Lenses, Two Different Questions

People often ask "Qimen or Bazi — which is more accurate?" It is the wrong question. They are not
competing answers to the same question; they are two lenses pointing at different things.

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/qimen-vs-bazi.html). This is a condensed version.*

## Bazi sees "who you tend to be"

A Bazi (Four Pillars) chart is cast once, from your birth moment, and never changes. It maps
**tendencies**: temperament, what you are drawn to, where you tend to get stuck, which elements run
hot or cold in your life. It is a portrait, not a forecast.

Use it when the question is about *pattern*: "Why do I keep ending up in the same kind of
relationship?" "What kind of work actually fits me?"

## Qi Men Dunjia sees "what is happening right now"

A Qimen chart is cast for **this moment and this question**. It maps a current situation: who is
active and who is passive, where the openings are, where the blocks sit, and which timing windows
are live. Cast the same question tomorrow and the chart changes, because the situation has moved.

Use it when the question is about *action*: "Should I sign this contract this week?" "Is now the
time to approach this person?"

## Side by side, it looks more like you

| | Bazi | Qi Men Dunjia |
|---|---|---|
| Cast from | Birth moment | The moment of the question |
| Changes over time | No | Yes, every two hours |
| Answers | Who you tend to be | What is happening now |
| Best for | Self-understanding | A specific decision |

Read alone, each is half a picture. Bazi tells you the kind of sailor you are; Qimen tells you the
weather on this crossing. You need both to decide whether to sail today.

## The operational difference

- **Bazi:** one chart, a lifetime of reference. Do not re-cast it for every question.
- **Qimen:** one chart per question, cast at the moment you ask. Do not reuse last week's chart for
  a new decision.

## FAQ

**Which is more accurate?** They measure different things, so accuracy is not comparable. Bazi is
judged by how well the tendencies fit your life; Qimen by whether the read structure matches the
situation you are actually in.

**Which should I look at first?** Bazi for the pattern, Qimen for the move. If you are choosing
between two paths this month, Qimen is the operative lens.

**Does the Qimen chart change?** Yes — every two hours (one 时辰), and it is recast per question.
That is the point: it tracks a moving situation.

**Will a tool tell me what to choose?** No honest one will. A good reading shows the structure —
where the door is, whether it is open, what blocks it — and leaves the decision with you.
