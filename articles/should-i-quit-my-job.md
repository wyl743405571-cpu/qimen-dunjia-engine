# Should I Quit My Job? Reading the Decision with Qi Men Dunjia

"Should I quit?" is not one question — it is a bundle of fear, timing, money, and identity. A Qimen
chart cannot resolve the bundle, but it can separate it. Here is a four-step way to read a
quit-or-stay decision without handing your agency to a tool.

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/should-i-quit-my-job-qimen.html). This is a condensed version.*

## The rider between two doors

In Qimen, career and livelihood sit on two different doors: **开门 (Open Door)** for position,
authority, and the public role; **生门 (Life Door)** for income, resources, and what actually sustains
you. The person deciding whether to leave a job is standing between them — and the two do not always
point the same way. A role can look like a promotion on the Open Door while draining the Life Door.

## Step 1 — Turn the dilemma into one specific question

"Is quitting right?" cannot be read. "Is the next three months the right window to move from this
role to that direction?" can. Name the direction, name the window, name what you are actually
leaving for.

## Step 2 — Cast the chart for this moment

Cast for the moment you ask — not for your birth time. The chart tracks the situation, and the
situation is now.

## Step 3 — Read the structure, not a verdict

Look at three things before any interpretation:

- **The state of 开门 and 生门** in relation to your palace — is the door open, blocked, or void?
- **The Day Stem (日干)** — where *you* sit in the chart, and what is pressing on you.
- **伏吟 (stagnation) and 空亡 (void)** — a stagnant chart argues for holding position; a hollow
  palace argues that what looks like an option may not be real yet.

The output is a structure: *where the movement is, where the resistance is, which window is live*.
Not "quit" or "stay".

## Step 4 — The chart reflects structure; the decision stays yours

This is the part that keeps the method honest. A chart can tell you the door is open; it cannot tell
you whether you want to walk through it. Financial runway, family, health, and what you actually want
are not in the chart — they are in you.

## What this method is, and what it claims

Qimen is a **decision-structuring tool**, not an oracle. It is useful precisely because it forces the
fuzzy question into a specific one and shows the symbols behind each claim. If a reading tells you
"just quit" without showing you the palace it read, it is not a reading — it is a horoscope with
better branding.
