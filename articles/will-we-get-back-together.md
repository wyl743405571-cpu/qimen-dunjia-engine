# Will We Get Back Together? What a Qi Men Chart Can and Cannot Tell You

The reconciliation question is the most common thing people bring to a Qimen chart — and the easiest
place for a reading to become dishonest, because the person asking is usually hoping for one specific
answer. Here is the honest version.

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/will-we-get-back-together-qimen.html). This is a condensed version.*

## First, what kind of chart this is

A relationship question needs a clean frame. Are you asking about **your own position** in the
bond, or about **the bond itself**? The chart maps a situation's structure — not another person's
private thoughts, and certainly not their free will.

If the question is framed as "will they come back", the honest reframe is: "what is the current
structure of this connection, and where is my own opening?"

## What the chart can tell you

- **Who is active, who is passive** in the current structure (who holds the initiative right now).
- **Where the channel is open or blocked** — 开门 and 休门 in relation to your palace, 空亡 marking
  what is hollow rather than real.
- **Timing windows** — which periods carry movement, which are stagnant (伏吟).
- **Your own leverage** — what you control, what you are waiting on someone else for.

## What the chart cannot tell you

- It cannot guarantee reconciliation.
- It cannot read the other person's mind or override their choice.
- It cannot replace an actual conversation.
- It cannot tell you to wait indefinitely — a chart showing stagnation is information, not a promise.

## The frame that keeps this honest: reflect, don't judge

Qimen works as a **mirror of structure**, not a verdict. The useful output is not "yes" or "no" but
a clearer picture of what you are actually dealing with: where the bond is real, where it is hollow,
and whether your waiting is a strategy or an avoidance.

## Three questions before any chart

1. What exactly do you want — the person, the version of the relationship you remember, or relief
   from the uncertainty?
2. What would you accept if they returned *unchanged*?
3. What are you avoiding by focusing on them instead of your own next move?

Answer those first. A chart read without them tends to tell you what you want to hear.
