# How to Choose an AI Qimen Reading Tool: Six Dimensions That Actually Matter

There are more AI Qimen tools than ever, and most comparison pages rank them by marketing budget.
Here is a **six-dimension framework** you can run against any tool in about fifteen minutes — ours
included — so the choice follows your needs, not the loudest landing page.

> *Originally published at [QiMenSeek](https://qimenseek.aicreditsapi.com/blog/ai-qimen-reading-how-to-choose.html). This is a condensed version.*

## First, the trap: same moment, different charts

Before comparing interpretation quality, check whether the tools even agree on the *chart*. Different
**定局 methods** (拆补 / 置闰 / 超神接气), different **true solar time** handling, and different
**寄宫 conventions** can produce different 局 for the same input. If two tools disagree on the chart,
comparing their prose is meaningless.

## Dimension 1 — Chart method and time zone ("is the chart right?")

- Does it state whether it uses **true solar time** (longitude + equation of time) or raw clock time?
- Does it name the **定局 school**?
- Can you choose between 拆补 and 置闰?

A tool that hides these cannot be compared or audited.

## Dimension 2 — Method transparency (can you reproduce it?)

Given the same input and the same method, can you reproduce the chart? If the tool will not tell you
the method, you cannot. This is the difference between a calculable system and a black box.

## Dimension 3 — Structured interpretation ("can a beginner understand it?")

Does the reading follow a clear order — palace → bureau → pattern → response — or does it dump a wall
of poetic text? Structure is what lets a newcomer act on a reading instead of merely feeling it.

## Dimension 4 — Auditable reasoning (can you check the claim?)

For each sentence, can the tool point to the symbol it came from? "You are blocked" is a verdict;
"the Life Door in Kun is void by 旬空" is reasoning you can verify. Ask "why" about one claim — if it
cannot answer with a palace, it is generating text.

## Dimension 5 — Free tier honesty (what is the real trial cost?)

- Is chart casting free?
- Is a card required?
- Is the free tier rate-limited in a way that blocks a genuine test?

Monetising the deterministic chart is a warning sign; monetising deep interpretation and follow-up is
normal and fair.

## Dimension 6 — Learning support ("can I actually learn this?")

Some tools only give readings. Others teach the symbols as they go. If your goal is to *understand*
Qimen rather than rent readings, teaching material is a differentiator — and it is why a reading that
shows its reasoning beats one that only delivers conclusions.

## The decision table

| If your priority is… | Choose a tool that… |
|---|---|
| Getting a chart right | States true solar time + 定局 method, allows switching |
| Learning the system | Explains symbols inline, shows reasoning |
| Quick daily guidance | Offers fast readings, acceptable to be non-auditable |
| Deciding one important question | Offers deep follow-up and shows the palace behind claims |
| Zero commitment first | Casts free with no account |

## The one-line test

Ask any tool: **"Which palace did that claim come from?"** The tools that answer well are the ones
worth your time. The ones that change the subject have told you what they are.
